Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about Safe Space

Is Safe Space a replacement for therapy? +

No, Safe Space is not a replacement for professional therapy. It's designed as a supportive tool for everyday mental wellbeing, providing a listening ear and helpful resources. For serious mental health concerns, we always recommend consulting with a qualified professional.

How does Safe Space protect my privacy? +

Your privacy is our top priority. All conversations are encrypted end-to-end. We don't store personally identifiable information with your conversations, and you can delete your chat history at any time. We adhere to strict data protection regulations.

Will Safe Space be free to use? +

Yes! We believe mental health support should be accessible to everyone. Safe Space will have a free tier with core features. We may offer premium features in the future to support development, but the essential support functions will always remain free.

What age group is Safe Space designed for? +

Safe Space is designed for young people aged 13 and above. The language and support approaches are tailored to be accessible and helpful for teenagers and young adults. For younger children, we recommend parental guidance and age-appropriate resources.

How is the AI trained to be empathetic? +

Our AI is trained on thousands of supportive conversations supervised by mental health professionals. It incorporates principles of active listening, validation, and evidence-based therapeutic techniques. We continuously refine it based on feedback from psychologists and users.
