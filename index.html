Youth Mental Health Facts

Understanding the reality to create better support

1

in 7 Adolescents Experience Mental Health Conditions

Globally, approximately 14–15% of 10–19‑year‑olds have a mental disorder

Source: World Health Organization
75%

Begin by Age 24

Three-quarters of all lifetime mental disorders start by age 24, with half beginning by age 14

Source: UNICEF & Mental Health Foundation
Leading

Cause of Disability in Youth

Mental disorders are among the leading causes of illness and disability in adolescents

Source: JAMA Network

These facts highlight the urgent need for accessible mental health support for young people. Early intervention and compassionate care can make a profound difference.
