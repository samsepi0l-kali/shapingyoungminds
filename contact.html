All communications are encrypted and confidential. We adhere to strict privacy policies and professional ethical guidelines. Your information is only used to provide you with appropriate support.

Common Questions

Answers to frequently asked questions

We respond to all inquiries within 24 hours on weekdays. Weekend messages receive responses by Monday afternoon. Support requests marked as "High Urgency" receive priority attention.

Initial consultations are always free. Some specialized services may have associated costs, which will be clearly communicated upfront with absolutely no pressure to proceed.

You can use initials or a pseudonym in initial communications. However, for ongoing support, we'll need verified contact information to ensure safe and ethical service delivery.

We support children (5+), adolescents, and young adults up to age 25. We also provide guidance and resources for parents and caregivers of all ages.

Currently, all our services are provided online through secure video conferencing, phone calls, or email. This allows us to reach families across different locations while maintaining high standards of care.
