About Dr. Deepika

Prof. Dr. Deepika Chamoli Shahi is a compassionate Cognitive Psychologist, Clinical Hypnotherapist, and Past Life Regression (PLR) expert with over 22 years of experience in supporting mental and emotional well-being. Recognised among the top online psychologists (2021–2022), she has been a pioneer in making quality mental health care accessible through online counselling.

Her contributions have also been honoured with the Women Science Communicator Award by the State Minister of Uttarakhand, recognizing her dedication to making mental health support accessible and understandable to everyone.
